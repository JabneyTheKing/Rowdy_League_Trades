Updated 2026-10-17T10:58:18.174535+00:00; through Week 5.

**Estimate, not actual fantasy points.** The original Team Impact report is unchanged. The same four roster scenarios and legal starting slots are reoptimized with injury estimates applied equally in every scenario.

Only completed-week, zero-participation absences supported by a dated NFL weekly injury OUT report or the first four team games after a documented NFL Reserve/Injured placement qualify. Byes and unplayed games are excluded. Current injury labels are never applied backward. An absence is replaced by that player’s mean league points in at least two earlier games with recorded participation and no injury listing in the archived weekly report. No future games or previous-season points are used. This is an observable participation proxy, not a guarantee of perfect health. In-game injuries, reduced snaps, PUP/NFI, unverified absences, and longer IR absences without fresh OUT evidence are not adjusted. Players without a qualifying baseline stay at actual points and are listed below. Confirmed absence coverage is conservative, so this is not a fully healthy-team simulation.

Wire impact = wire-only minus draft. Trade impact = all-moves minus wire-only, including interaction. The two sum to total improvement. Recorded moves are held fixed; hypothetical caps/budgets and alternate decisions are not modeled. Only fantasy roster moves from August 23 onward change ownership. Standalone drops affect availability but are not separately scored.
